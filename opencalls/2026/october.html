15 Oct 2026 CFP: Beyond Gentrification: Performing Spatial Transformation
  • Full URL https://iftr.org/conference-cfp-beyond-gentrification-performing-spatial-transformation/
  • Deadline 15 October 2026
  • Tags

June 24-25, 2027, Seminarraum 1+2, Austrian Academy of Sciences, Vienna

This conference seeks to build on this budding interdisciplinary conversation to re-examine the relationship of theatre and performance to spatial history, development and transformation. Rather than taking the question of agency or causality as a given, however, we seek to think expansively about the many roles that theatre and performance play in altering, developing, and negotiating both urban and non-urban space. While open to scholars working across Theatre and Performance Studies and Urban Studies, we also intend for our use of the term “performance” to function expansively, and invite proposals that examine a range of cultural forms, including dance, music, festivals, art biennials, and nightlife.

ESEH / University of Salzburg - Salzburg, Austria, 7–10 September 2027

The European Society for Environmental History (ESEH) is pleased to invite proposals for panel sessions, individual papers, book presentations, posters, roundtables, and more experimental presentation formats for its upcoming biennial conference. The conference theme – Environmental History Beyond Disciplinary Boundaries – aims to highlight and reinforce the interdisciplinary and transdisciplinary nature and culture of environmental history.
